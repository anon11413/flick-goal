Flick Goal Privacy Policy

In short
  • Flick Goal has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress (coins, best scores, unlocked items, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID and other device data to show and measure ads.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play; we use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and restore "No Ads". We never see your payment details.

1. Who we are

Flick Goal ("the app", "we") is a casual football kicking game for Android, published on Google Play by [DEVELOPER NAME]. For any privacy question or request, email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

2. Data stored on your device

The app keeps your game data in the app's local storage on your phone: coins, best scores and distances, owned and equipped balls and stadiums, upgrades, tutorial progress, sound / vibration settings, how many games you have played, when ads were last shown (to limit how often they appear), and the IDs of purchases that already gave you coins (so a purchase is never counted twice). This data is not sent to us. Android may include it in your device's own backup (Google's Android backup service) if you have backups turned on. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads through Google AdMob: an optional "watch a video" ad (for example to continue a run or double your coins) and, from time to time, a full-screen ad between games. Buying "No Ads" removes the full-screen ads; the optional video ads stay available because you choose to watch them.

To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • IP address, from which an approximate location (for example the country or city) can be derived;
  • App interactions: app launches, ad views, taps on ads and video views;
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data from the ads SDK.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Data is encrypted in transit.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or a region with similar rules, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and a "Privacy & ad choices" button in the game's Settings lets you change your choice at any time. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the wording varies by phone). You still see ads, but they are less personalized. You can also buy "No Ads".

4. In-app purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

You can buy coin packs and "No Ads". Payments are handled entirely by Google Play under your Google account and the Google Payments privacy notice. We never receive your card or bank details.

We use RevenueCat as our purchase service provider to verify purchases with Google Play, keep track of which products you own (so "No Ads" can be restored after reinstalling) and report sales totals to us. For this, the app sends RevenueCat a random app user ID that it creates (not linked to your name or email), your purchase history for this app (product, time, price, currency and store transaction identifiers), and basic device and app information (such as app version, OS version, country or storefront, and IP address, which RevenueCat uses to process the request). See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Coin packs are used up when you buy them, so Google Play cannot restore them on a new phone; "No Ads" is restored with the "Restore Purchases" button in Settings, and automatically when you reinstall with the same Google account.

5. What we do NOT do

  • No account, login, chat, social features or user-generated content.
  • No precise (GPS) location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use analytics of our own inside the app.

6. Children

Flick Goal is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to content rated for general and parental-guidance audiences. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Keeping and deleting data

Game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as needed to provide purchases, restore them and meet legal and tax obligations. Because the app has no accounts, we cannot identify you from your email alone: to ask for access to or deletion of purchase data held by our providers, email [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] with your Google Play order number (from your Google Play purchase receipt), and we will pass the request on. For advertising data, use Google's controls at My Ad Center and reset your advertising ID.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Contact [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] to use these rights.
